Travel Info

Helpful details for planning a smooth visit.

Currency

Taniti uses the U.S. dollar. Many businesses also accept euros and yen.

Language

Many younger Tanitians speak fluent English, while less English is spoken in rural areas.

Healthcare

There is one hospital and several clinics. The hospital has many multilingual employees.

Safety

Violent crime is very rare, though pickpocketing and other petty crimes have increased as tourism grows.

National Holidays

Many tourist attractions and restaurants close on national holidays.

Power

Power outlets are 120 volts, the same as in the United States.
