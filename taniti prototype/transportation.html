Transportation

Getting to Taniti and getting around the island.

Air Travel

Most visitors arrive by air at Taniti’s small airport.

Public Buses

Public buses serve Taniti City from 5 a.m. to 11 p.m. every day.

Taxis

Taxis are available in Taniti City.

Rental Cars

Rental cars are available from an agency near the airport.

Bikes

Bikes and helmets can be rented from several vendors. Helmets are required by law.

Walking

Taniti City is fairly flat and walkable, and Merriton Landing is easy to explore on foot.
