FAQs

Frequently requested travel information.

What currency is used?

The U.S. dollar is Taniti’s currency.

Can I rent a car?

Yes. Rental cars are available near the airport.

What are the bus hours?

Public buses run from 5 a.m. to 11 p.m. in Taniti City.

Is Taniti walkable?

Taniti City and Merriton Landing are easy to explore on foot.
