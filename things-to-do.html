Things to Do

Adventure, nature, sightseeing, and island culture.

Beaches

Enjoy the white sandy beaches around Yellow Leaf Bay.

Volcano

Explore Taniti’s mountainous interior and active volcano.

Rainforest

Popular options include rainforest hikes and zip-lining.

Water Activities

Snorkeling and chartered fishing tours are available.

Sightseeing

Boat and bus tours offer additional ways to explore the island.

Entertainment

Visitors can also find a museum, pubs, a microbrewery, a dance club, helicopter rides, an arcade, art galleries, and bowling.
